How it works.

Four steps, and you see working versions from the second one on.

  1. A 30-minute call

    What decision the tool should support, who will use it, and what data you already have.

  2. Scope and quote

    A short written scope: what you get, what it needs from you, the timeline and the price.

  3. Build in rounds

    You see working versions early and often, on your own data where you allow it.

  4. Handover

    The files, a short guide and a walkthrough, so your team can run it without me.

Questions.

What do you need from us to start?

A sample export of the data (anonymised is fine), the decision the tool should support, and one person who can answer questions.

Do we need Power BI or other special software?

No. I build in what you already use: Excel, Power BI, Google Sheets, or a web page that opens in any browser.

Do you work with organisations outside Pakistan?

Yes, remotely. I am in Islamabad (UTC+5) and work across time zones by call and email.

Who owns what you build?

You do, once the project is paid: the files, the source and the guide.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the data and the scope. You get a timeline with the quote, before anything starts.
